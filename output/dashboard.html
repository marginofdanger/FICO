VantageScore 4.0 adoption across Fannie Mae & Freddie Mac

How fast lenders are delivering loans scored by VantageScore 4.0 instead of Classic FICO, measured at issuance from both GSEs' loan-level disclosure — the leading edge of FHFA's credit-score model change.

Source: Fannie + Freddie loan-level disclosure (mbs-securities.com) Scope: single-class SF, loan-level (each loan once) Through: June 2026

Freddie Mac moved first and stays ahead. Total agency VantageScore penetration is zero through March 2026, then Freddie posts the first loans in April (Fannie follows in May), reaching 0.84% of combined UPB in June ($559M, 1,336 loans, 423 pools). Freddie 0.89% vs Fannie 0.80%. Real, but still a sliver — and delivered 100% standalone: no loan carries both a FICO and a VantageScore.
Sources & method.
  1. Data — loan-level, straight from the GSE disclosure portals. Every figure is derived from the month-end single-family MBS loan-level issuance files: Fannie Mae via PoolTalk® (fanniemae.mbs-securities.com, files FNM_ILLD) and Freddie Mac via its disclosure site (freddiemac.mbs-securities.com, files FRE_ILLD), Dec 2025–Jun 2026. These are loan-level records (one row per loan) — issuance, penetration, the cross-tab, product mix, and the lender view all come from this single dataset (no pool-level rollups or third-party data), so every view reconciles. Regulatory dates verified against FHFA.
  2. Fields & categories. Per loan we read Classic FICO / VantageScore 4.0 (plus the Origination variants for modified/reperforming loans), Seller Name, loan term, and issuance UPB. A loan "has" a score if it is a valid 300–850 value. VS-scored = VS-only; zero loans carry both — consistent with FHFA, which will not accept a loan delivered with more than one score model.
  3. Denominators & levels. Base = all newly-issued SF loans that month (loan-level count and UPB). Penetration = VS-only ÷ base. Lender-level: each loan attributed to its Seller Name; a lender's rate = its VS loans ÷ its own loans; market share = its loans ÷ all agency loans. Product = by loan term; "pools" = distinct security identifiers.

The pilot. FHFA authorized VantageScore 4.0 for GSE loans on Jul 8, 2025; the disclosure fields went live Nov 17, 2025; and the interim phase — approved lenders may deliver a loan under either Classic FICO or VantageScore 4.0 — opened Apr 22, 2026 to a limited set of approved lenders (reported to include Rocket Mortgage, PennyMac, NewRez and roughly 18 other large originators). Being an approved participant is not the same as delivering VantageScore — e.g., PennyMac is on the list but shows ~0 VS deliveries through June. FICO Score 10T historical data was published Jul 1, 2026, with adoption to follow.

Important — the score shown is the lender's choice, not the only score pulled. The GSEs still require a three-bureau tri-merge credit report, so a VantageScore-delivered loan almost always also had FICO scores pulled at underwriting. Under lender choice, the originator sees both models and submits whichever it selects as the qualifying score (frequently the one giving better eligibility or pricing). So "VantageScore-only" here means the model the lender chose to deliver to Fannie/Freddie — not that the borrower has no FICO.
